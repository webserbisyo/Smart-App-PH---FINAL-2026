# Sales kit (phase 9, T118 task 9.3)

Words for selling Smart App PH to the first shops. Plain English with local words where natural, short lines,
nothing promised that the app does not do today. The founder records the video and sends the replies.

Facts every line below rests on (change them here first if they change):

- One plan, Pro: **₱399 a month**, or **₱1,995 for 6 months** (a month free). Super Admin › Setup holds the live
  numbers.
- **14-day free trial** from the day we approve the shop. No card, no payment details at sign-up.
- Owners pay us by **GCash or Maya** and send the receipt photo in Settings › Plan. We check it by hand,
  usually the same day.
- Customers order from the shop's own link (`<shop>.webserbisyo.com`), installable like an app. They pay the
  shop directly (GCash, Maya, QR Ph, cash). We never touch the shop's money.
- Members and the stamp card are the main reason to switch: customers join with their email, collect stamps,
  and get the prize the owner picked.

## 2. Messenger replies

Use Super Admin › Setup › "Sample shops to send" for the links; its "Copy the reply" button copies the first
message below with every link filled in.

**When someone asks "Ano 'to?" or "How much?"**

> Hi! Smart App PH gives your shop its own ordering app with a stamp card, so customers order from you directly
> and keep coming back. Here are sample shops you can try, just like your customers would: (links)
> It's free for 14 days, then ₱399 a month. Want me to help you set it up?

**When they say yes**

> Great! Sign up here: (sign-up link). It takes about 5 minutes: your name, email, then your shop's name and
> type. Add 5 items, your logo, your hours and how customers pay, then tap Ask to go live. I'll check it and
> turn it on the same day.

**When they want help setting up**

> No problem. Send me your menu (a photo is fine), your logo and your GCash or Maya number, and I'll set it up
> with you. You'll get an email to make your password.

**When they ask about payment to us**

> After the trial you pay ₱399 a month (or ₱1,995 for 6 months) by GCash or Maya. In the app, open Settings,
> Plan, send the amount, and upload the receipt. I'll confirm it in the Billing chat there.

**When they are not ready**

> Sure, no rush. The sample shops stay open if you want to look again: (all sample shops link).

## 3. FAQ

**How much is it?** ₱399 a month, or ₱1,995 for 6 months. One plan with everything; no fee per order.

**Is there a free trial?** Yes, 14 days from the day your shop goes live. No card needed.

**How do my customers pay?** Directly to you, by GCash, Maya, QR Ph, bank transfer or cash. For transfers they
send a screenshot in the order, and you check it.

**Do you take a cut of my sales?** No. The monthly plan is the only fee.

**Do my customers need to download an app?** No. They open your link; they can add it to their home screen
like an app.

**Can I do delivery?** Yes. You set fees by distance or by area, and you book the rider yourself.

**What happens if I stop paying?** We send reminders before the end date. After a short grace period the shop
stops taking new orders until you pay; your items, members and history stay.

**Who owns my customers' details?** You do. We keep them for your shop only and never sell them. Our privacy
notice explains what we keep and why.

**Do you share data with Facebook?** Only about people signing up for Smart App PH itself (to measure our own
ads), and only in a scrambled (hashed) form. Nothing about your customers or their orders is sent.

**Can I cancel?** Yes, anytime. Ask in the Billing chat; we close the shop on the day you choose.
