# Demo images: the answer key for T02a

Source: Demo Shop Assets v1.2 (Google Doc, approved A1 to A9). 105 images: 83 products, 15 banners, 5 logos, 2 custom prizes. Wordmarks stay in Drive. Halo Dessert Bar is MVP B only (A9) and never goes into these folders.

Use this list to identify each image the founder generated: match what the picture shows to the "Looks like" column, then write the output files into `apps/storefront/public/demo/<shop>/`. Output names use one pattern, `<name>-<width>.<ext>`, so `imageUrl()` needs no special cases.

Sizes (A6): products and prizes 640 and 320 px square (about 40 KB at 640); banners 1600 × 900 and 800 × 450 (about 120 KB at 1600); logos 256 and 128 px; app icons PNG 192, 512, maskable 512 (logo inside the middle 80 percent on the brand colour) and 180 (apple-touch). Also `brand/og.jpg` 1200 × 630, under 150 KB, cropped from banner 1, no text.
